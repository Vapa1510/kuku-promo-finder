## 🛠️ Pre-Recording Checklist (30-Second Setup)

1. **Open Studio:** Open your live Vercel URL (or `http://localhost:3000` via `npm start`) in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
2. **Full Screen:** Press **`F11`** to enter clean full-screen mode with zero browser clutter.
3. **Capture Settings:** In your screen recorder (OBS Studio, Loom, or Windows Game Bar `Win + G`):
   - Set resolution to **1080p (1920×1080)** at 60 FPS.
   - Verify **Microphone Audio** is active.
   - Verify **System Audio** is enabled (so the procedural heartbeat pulse and clock ticks are recorded when you click Play).
4. **Pacing:** Aim for a steady, confident conversational pace of ~130 to 140 words per minute.
